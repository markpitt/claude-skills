# GCSE History 2026 Specification Updates

## Overview

Several exam boards have made content changes for students taking GCSE History exams in 2026. This file documents what has changed, what has been removed, and what is new — so tutors can ensure students are not revising content that is no longer examined.

---

## Edexcel (Pearson) — Issue 6 Specification Updates

Edexcel published Issue 6 of its GCSE History specification with targeted refinements for 2026. No significant chunks of content have been removed and no major new content has been added. The changes focus on clarifying scope and removing outdated terminology.

### Nature of the Changes
- **Clarification of scope:** Some topic descriptions have been reworded to flag where excessive depth is not required
- **Chronological reordering:** Some content moved to create a more logical teaching sequence
- **Simplification:** Unnecessary detail removed from specific topics
- **Language sensitivity:** Outdated or problematic terminology updated throughout

### Updated Terminology
| Old Term | Replacement |
|----------|-------------|
| "Slaves" | "Enslaved persons" |
| "Plains Indians" | "Native Americans" or specific indigenous group names |

### Specific Content Removals / Simplifications by Topic

**Medicine in Britain (Paper 1):**
- Hippocrates has been **removed** from the specification
- Florence Nightingale's influence is now focused **solely on Britain** (previously broader)
- For the Western Front historic environment: **trench construction** and **Hill 60** have been removed

**Anglo-Saxon and Norman England (Paper 2):**
- Anglo-Saxon resistance events are now studied **collectively** rather than individually
- The "rising against Tostig" is now focused **strictly on causation** only

**Henry VIII and his Ministers (Paper 2):**
- The influence of **the Seymours** (aside from Jane Seymour) has been removed
- **Reasons for the failure of the Pilgrimage of Grace** have been removed

**Early Elizabethan England (Paper 2):**
- **Consequences of the Spanish Armada** have been removed
- Study of **universities** has been removed

**Weimar and Nazi Germany (Paper 3):**
- **Early growth and features of the Nazi party** no longer need to be studied in depth
- The specific roles of **Brüning and von Schleicher in 1932** no longer need to be studied in depth

**Superpower Relations and the Cold War (Paper 2):**
- The **1987 INF Treaty** has been removed from the specification

**The USA, 1954–75 (Paper 3):**
- **Dien Bien Phu and the end of French rule in Vietnam** have been removed

## AQA — 2026 Changes

AQA has made minimal content changes for 2026. The primary update for AQA students is the **rotation of Historic Environment sites**.

### 2026 Historic Environment Sites

These sites are examined in Paper 2, Section B (British Depth Study). The specific site depends on which British depth study the student's school has chosen:

| British Depth Study | 2026 Historic Environment Site |
|--------------------|-------------------------------|
| Norman England, c1066–c1100 | **Pevensey Castle** |
| Medieval England, the reign of Edward I, 1272–1307 | **Caernarfon Castle** |
| Elizabethan England, c1568–1603 | **The Globe Theatre** |
| Restoration England, 1660–1685 | **Ham House** |

**What students need to know about their site:**
- Physical layout and key features of the site today
- How the site has changed over time
- How the site relates to the political, social, or cultural history of the period
- What the site reveals that written sources alone cannot
- What historians and archaeologists have said about the site

**Important:** The site changes every year. Students sitting in 2027 will study completely different sites. Students must revise *this year's* designated site, not a previous year's.

## OCR — 2026 Changes

OCR has not made major specification changes for 2026 exams.

**OCR History B (Schools History Project) — History Around Us Component:**
There is no centrally designated historic site for this component. Schools choose their own approved local or national site and submit it to OCR via a Site Proposal Form. Each school's students study a different site — students should check with their teacher which site has been chosen at their school.

## Eduqas / WJEC — 2026 Changes

### Eduqas (England) — 2026–27 Thematic Study Historic Sites

The historic sites linked to Eduqas Thematic Studies change on a two-year cycle. For the **2026 and 2027 summer examination series**:

| Thematic Study | Historic Site |
|----------------|--------------|
| Changes in Crime and Punishment | Policing in Liverpool, c.1787–1900 |
| Changes in Health and Medicine | Letchworth Garden City, c.1903–present |
| The Development of Warfare | Royal Dockyards, Chatham, c.1567–1984 |
| Changes in Entertainment and Leisure | Glastonbury Festival, c.1970–present |

### WJEC "Made for Wales" — New Specification

A radically redesigned GCSE History qualification is being introduced in Wales from **September 2026**, with first examinations in **summer 2027**. This new qualification features:
- Heavy use of Non-Examination Assessment (coursework)
- Strong focus on Welsh history, diversity, and Wales's place in the world
- On-screen digital assessment for some components
- Four Units replacing the previous structure

**This affects only schools in Wales following the WJEC "Made for Wales" specification.** Schools in England using Eduqas are not affected.

# GCSE History Curriculum Overview (2026)

## Assessment Objectives (All Boards)

Ofqual sets consistent AOs across all English GCSE History boards:

| AO | Description | Weight |
|----|-------------|--------|
| AO1 | Knowledge and understanding of key features and characteristics of periods studied | 35% |
| AO2 | Explain and analyse historical events using second-order concepts (causation, consequence, similarity, difference, change, continuity, significance) | 35% |
| AO3 | Analyse, evaluate, and use contemporary sources to make substantiated judgements | 15% |
| AO4 | Analyse, evaluate, and make substantiated judgements about historical interpretations | 15% |

## AQA History (8145)

**Structure:** Two 2-hour papers, each worth 50% (84 marks each, including 4 SPaG marks)

### Paper 1: Understanding the Modern World

**Section A — Period Studies (choose one):**
- America 1840–1895: Expansion and Consolidation
- America 1920–1973: Opportunity and Inequality
- Germany 1890–1945: Democracy and Dictatorship
- Russia 1894–1945: Tsardom and Communism

**Section B — Wider World Depth Studies (choose one):**
- Conflict and Tension: First World War, 1894–1918
- Conflict and Tension: Inter-War Years, 1918–1939
- Conflict and Tension: East and West, 1945–1972 (Cold War)
- Conflict and Tension in Asia, 1950–1975
- Conflict and Tension: The Gulf, 1990–2009

### Paper 2: Shaping the Nation

**Section A — Thematic Studies (choose one):**
- Health and the People: c1000–present day
- Power and the People: c1170–present day
- Migration, Empires and the People: c790–present day

**Section B — British Depth Studies + Historic Environment (choose one):**
| Option | 2026 Historic Environment Site |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| Norman England, c1066–c1100 | Pevensey Castle |
| Medieval England, the reign of Edward I, 1272–1307 | Caernarfon Castle |
| Elizabethan England, c1568–1603 | The Globe Theatre |
| Restoration England, 1660–1685 | Ham House |

| Component | Duration | Weight | Content |
|-----------|----------|--------|---------|

## Key Vocabulary: Second-Order Historical Concepts (AO2)

These are the conceptual lenses examiners expect students to use in analytical answers:

| Concept | What it means | Example question prompt |
|---------|--------------|------------------------|
| **Causation** | Why did something happen? | "Explain why..." |
| **Consequence** | What were the results? | "What were the effects of..." |
| **Change** | How did things develop over time? | "How did X change between..." |
| **Continuity** | What stayed the same? | "What remained the same?" |
| **Similarity** | How were things alike? | "In what ways were X and Y similar?" |
| **Difference** | How were things different? | "Compare X and Y..." |
| **Significance** | Why does this matter historically? | "How significant was...?" |

Students should use these concepts as analytical frameworks — not just describe events but ask *why* and *so what?*

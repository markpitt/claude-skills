# GCSE English Literature Curriculum Overview (2026)

---

## AQA GCSE English Literature (8702)

Closed-text, linear qualification. **No texts permitted in the exam hall.** Two written papers worth 160 marks total.

### Paper 1: Shakespeare and the 19th-Century Novel
**Duration:** 1 hour 45 minutes | **Marks:** 64 | **Weighting:** 40%

| Section | Text | Task | Marks |
|---------|------|------|-------|
| **A — Shakespeare** | One play from the set list | Extract provided on the paper + essay using whole play | 34 |
| **B — 19th-Century Novel** | One novel from the set list | Extract provided + whole-text essay | 30 |

- Section A: One essay question. Students are given a printed extract and must close-read it AND discuss the play as a whole.
- Section B: Two-part question. Part (i) is extract-based; part (ii) asks about the wider novel.

**Shakespeare set texts (choose one):** Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, The Tempest, The Merchant of Venice, Much Ado About Nothing, Julius Caesar

**19th-Century Novel set texts (choose one):** The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, A Christmas Carol, Great Expectations, Jane Eyre, Frankenstein, Pride and Prejudice, The Sign of Four

---

### Paper 2: Modern Texts and Poetry
**Duration:** 2 hours 15 minutes | **Marks:** 96 | **Weighting:** 60%

| Section | Text | Task | Marks |
|---------|------|------|-------|
| **A — Modern Prose or Drama** | One modern text | Whole-text essay (no extract provided — closed book) | 34 |
| **B — Poetry Anthology** | One cluster (15 poems) | One named poem printed + compare to a second poem of choice from same cluster | 30 |
| **C — Unseen Poetry** | Unseen poem(s) | Analyse an unseen poem, then compare to a second unseen poem | 32 |

**Modern Drama set texts (choose one):** An Inspector Calls, Blood Brothers, The History Boys, DNA, The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night-Time, A Taste of Honey, Leave Taking, The Princess and the Hustler

**Modern Prose set texts (choose one):** Lord of the Flies, Animal Farm, Never Let Me Go, Anita and Me, Pigeon English, My Name is Leon, Telling Tales (anthology)

**Poetry Anthology Clusters (choose one):**
- *Love and Relationships* (15 poems)
- *Power and Conflict* (15 poems)
- *Worlds and Lives* (15 poems)

## Edexcel GCSE English Literature (1ET0)

Closed-text, linear qualification. Two written papers, equally weighted.

### Paper 1: Shakespeare and Post-1914 Literature
**Duration:** 1 hour 45 minutes | **Marks:** 80 | **Weighting:** 50%

- **Section A (Shakespeare):** Essay with two parts — close analysis of a printed extract + linking ideas to the whole play.
- **Section B (Post-1914 text):** Whole-text essay question. No extract — closed book.

**Shakespeare set texts (choose one):** Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, Much Ado About Nothing, The Merchant of Venice, Twelfth Night, The Tempest

**Post-1914 set texts (choose one):** An Inspector Calls, Hobson's Choice, Blood Brothers, Journey's End, Animal Farm, Lord of the Flies, Anita and Me, The Woman in Black, The Empress, Refugee Boy, Coram Boy, Boys Don't Cry
- **2026 new additions (first examination):** *Klara and the Sun* (Kazuo Ishiguro), *Western Lane* (Chetna Maroo)

---

### Paper 2: 19th-Century Novel and Poetry Since 1789
**Duration:** 2 hours 15 minutes | **Marks:** 80 | **Weighting:** 50%

- **Section A (19th-Century Novel):** Extract-based question + wider novel essay.
- **Section B (Poetry):**
  - Part 1: Compare a named anthology poem (printed) to a second poem of choice from the same cluster.
  - Part 2: Analyse an unseen poem, then compare to a second unseen poem.

**19th-Century Novel set texts (choose one):** Jane Eyre, Great Expectations, Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, A Christmas Carol, Pride and Prejudice, Silas Marner, Frankenstein

**Poetry Anthology Clusters (choose one):** *Relationships*, *Conflict*

## OCR GCSE English Literature (J352)

Closed-text, linear qualification. Two written papers, equally weighted at 50% each. Both papers are **2 hours long**, 80 marks each.

### Component 01: Exploring Modern and Literary Heritage Texts

- **Section A (Modern Prose or Drama):** Students compare themes/characters from their set text with an unseen modern extract of the same genre, then answer a related question on the set text.
- **Section B (19th-Century Prose):** Extract-based or whole-text essay question.

**Modern Prose/Drama set texts (choose one):** Animal Farm, Anita and Me, Never Let Me Go, DNA, My Mother Said I Never Should, Leave Taking
- Note: *Coriolanus*, *Twelfth Night*, and *Measure for Measure* are being **withdrawn** after June 2026.

**19th-Century Prose set texts (choose one):** Jane Eyre, Great Expectations, Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, A Christmas Carol, Pride and Prejudice

---

### Component 02: Exploring Poetry and Shakespeare

- **Section A (Poetry):** Compare an anthology poem to an unseen poem on the same theme + answer a related question on another anthology poem.
- **Section B (Shakespeare):** Extract-based or whole-play essay question.

**Shakespeare set texts (choose one):** Macbeth, Romeo and Juliet, The Merchant of Venice, Much Ado About Nothing

**Poetry Anthology Themes (choose one):** *Love and Relationships*, *Conflict*, *Youth and Age* (each containing 15 poems)

## Eduqas GCSE English Literature (Wales/England)

Closed-text, linear qualification. Two written components.

### Component 1: Shakespeare and Poetry
**Duration:** 2 hours | **Weighting:** 40%

- **Section A (Shakespeare):** One extract question + one whole-play essay question.
- **Section B (Poetry Anthology):** Two questions based on poems from the WJEC Eduqas Poetry Anthology, including a comparative question.

**Shakespeare set texts (choose one):** Romeo and Juliet, Macbeth, Othello, Much Ado About Nothing, Twelfth Night, The Merchant of Venice

**2026 Poetry Note:** Summer 2026 is the **final assessment series** for the current 18-poem WJEC Eduqas Poetry Anthology (includes *The Manhunt*, *Ozymandias*, *Dulce et Decorum Est*). A new 15-poem anthology will replace it from 2027.

---

### Component 2: Post-1914 Prose/Drama, 19th-Century Prose, and Unseen Poetry
**Duration:** 2 hours 30 minutes | **Weighting:** 60%

- **Section A (Post-1914 Prose/Drama):** Source-based response to a modern text.
- **Section B (19th-Century Prose):** Source-based response to a 19th-century text.
- **Section C (Unseen Poetry):** Two questions on unseen 20th/21st-century poems, one requiring comparison.

**Post-1914 set texts (choose one):** Lord of the Flies, Anita and Me, Boys Don't Cry, The Woman in Black, Oranges Are Not the Only Fruit, The Curious Incident of the Dog in the Night Time, Leave Taking, An Inspector Calls, The History Boys, Blood Brothers

**19th-Century Prose set texts (choose one):** A Christmas Carol, Silas Marner, The War of the Worlds, Pride and Prejudice, Jane Eyre, The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde

## Assessment Objectives Across All Boards

| AO | Description |
|----|-------------|
| **AO1** | Maintain a critical style; develop an informed personal response; use textual references strategically |
| **AO2** | Analyse language, form, and structure using relevant subject terminology to show how writers create meaning |
| **AO3** | Show understanding of the relationships between texts and contexts in which they were written |
| **AO4** | Use a range of vocabulary and sentence structures for clarity, purpose, and effect; error-free spelling and punctuation |

**Note:** AO4 (technical accuracy) applies to written exam responses. Students must write in formal academic English — precise vocabulary, no slang, no contractions.

## Common Set Text Choices (What Most Schools Pick)

While every school makes its own choice, the most widely taught texts for AQA are:

| Component | Most Common Choice |
|-----------|------------------|
| Shakespeare | *Macbeth* (by far the most popular) |
| 19th-Century Novel | *A Christmas Carol* or *Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* |
| Modern Drama | *An Inspector Calls* (most popular modern text overall) |
| Modern Prose | *Lord of the Flies* or *Animal Farm* |
| Poetry Cluster | *Power and Conflict* (more popular than Love and Relationships) |

When a student doesn't know which text their school chose, start with these defaults and confirm as early as possible.

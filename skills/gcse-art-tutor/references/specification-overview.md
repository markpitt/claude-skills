# GCSE Art and Design (Fine Art) — Specification Overview

## Course Structure

The GCSE is a **linear qualification** with two components, both internally assessed by teachers and externally moderated by the exam board:

| Component | Weighting | Description |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Component 1: Portfolio** | 60% | Sustained project showing creative journey from initial engagement to final outcome, plus a selection of further work. No time limit; internally set. |
| **Component 2: Externally Set Assignment (ESA)** | 40% | Response to a thematic starting point set by the exam board. Includes a preparatory period followed by a **10-hour supervised session** under exam conditions. |

## The Four Assessment Objectives (AOs)

All four AOs are **equally weighted at 25% each** across all UK exam boards. Every piece of work is assessed against all four simultaneously.

| AO | Title | Core Requirement |
|----|-------|-----------------|
| **AO1 — Develop** | Developing ideas through investigations, demonstrating critical understanding of sources | Research artists, movements, and contextual sources; show *how* they inform and connect to your own work |
| **AO2 — Refine** | Refining work by exploring ideas, selecting and experimenting with media, materials, techniques, and processes | Trial a wide range of media purposefully; refine and improve methods rather than random attempts |
| **AO3 — Record** | Recording ideas, observations, and insights relevant to intentions as work progresses | Primary observational drawing, original photography, purposeful written annotation linked to intentions |
| **AO4 — Present** | Presenting a personal and meaningful response that realises intentions and demonstrates understanding of visual language | The final outcome — must feel personally authored and clearly connect to all prior development |

## Mark Scheme: Six-Band Holistic Assessment

Work is assessed using a **best-fit** approach across six performance bands. Teachers look at the work as a whole and decide which band best describes it, then award a mark within that band.

| Band | Descriptor | Characteristics |
|------|------------|----------------|
| Band 6 | Exceptional ability | Convincingly/fully meets all AOs; highly personal, sophisticated, mature work |
| Band 5 | High ability | Mostly convincingly meets all AOs; strong independence and personal direction |
| Band 4 | Secure ability | Adequately meets all AOs; competent with some personal voice |
| Band 3 | Developing ability | Partially demonstrates AOs; inconsistent quality or depth |
| Band 2 | Limited ability | Emerging awareness of AOs; significant gaps in development or recording |
| Band 1 | Basic ability | Minimal demonstration of AOs; largely teacher-directed |

Within each band, markers award "just", "adequately/mostly", or "convincingly/fully" to position the mark.

## Exam Board Comparison (2026)

All boards share the same four AOs and 60/40 split but differ in raw mark totals and ESA structure:

### AQA (Specifications 8201 Art & Design / 8202 Fine Art)
- **Portfolio:** Marked out of 96 (24 marks per AO) — scaled x3
- **ESA:** Marked out of 96 (24 marks per AO) — scaled x2
- **Total scaled marks:** 480
- **ESA:** 7 distinct starting points (student chooses one)
- **2026 themes:** Nature, Isolation, Movement, Interiors, Time, Magnificent, Lost & Found

### Pearson Edexcel (Specification 1FA0 Fine Art / 1AD0 Art & Design)
- **Portfolio:** Marked out of 72 (18 marks per AO) — scaled x1.5
- **ESA:** Marked out of 72 (18 marks per AO) — scaled x1.0
- **Total scaled marks:** 180
- **ESA:** 1 broad theme with 5 sub-themes
- **2026 theme:** "Found" (sub-themes: People, Possessions, Human-made, Environment, Natural World)

### OCR (Specifications J170–J176)
- **Portfolio:** Marked out of 120 (30 marks per AO)
- **ESA:** Marked out of 80 (20 marks per AO)
- **Total marks:** 200
- **ESA:** 5 themes with written and visual stimuli
- **2026 themes:** Reflections, Connections, Woman, Layers, Twisting and Turning

### WJEC Eduqas (Specifications C650–C656 / C651 Fine Art)
- **Portfolio:** Marked out of 120 (30 marks per AO)
- **ESA:** Marked out of 80 (20 marks per AO)
- **Total marks:** 200
- **ESA:** 5 overarching "Big Ideas" (e.g. perspectives, belonging, collections, structures, protest, connection, identity, innovation, cynefin)

## Fine Art Endorsement vs Other Endorsements

All boards offer multiple endorsements (e.g. Graphic Communication, Photography, Textile Design). The Fine Art endorsement focuses on:

- **2D work:** drawing, painting, printmaking
- **3D work:** sculpture, ceramics, installation
- **Mixed media:** combining materials, collage, assemblage
- **Lens/light-based:** photography, digital media, film

Within Fine Art students must demonstrate breadth — showing skills across different media rather than relying on one approach throughout.

## Grade Boundaries

Grade boundaries are **not fixed** — they are set annually on results day to maintain standards and account for variation in difficulty. Students cannot know in advance where boundaries will fall. Aim for the highest possible quality across all four AOs, not for a minimum threshold.

## Submission and Moderation

- **Deadline:** Teachers submit marks and all work by **31 May 2026**
- **Moderation:** External moderators visit schools or review submitted samples in June 2026 to check marking standards
- **What moderators look at:** The entire body of work including sketchbooks, preparatory material, and final pieces from both components
